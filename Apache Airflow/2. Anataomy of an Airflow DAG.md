#### Tasks vs. operators

 In Airflow, operators have a single responsibility: they exist to
perform one single piece of work

- BashOperator 
- PythonOperator
- EmailOper


Tasks in Airflow manage the execution of an operator; they can be thought of as small wrappers around an operator or managers that ensure that the operator executes correctly. The wrapper role of a task involves handling errors, managing dependencies, and scheduling the execution of the operator.
