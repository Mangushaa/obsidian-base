# Correlate metric, trace, and log data

In Datadog, you can correlate metric, trace, and log data. This allows you to understand your infrastructure and applications at all levels and efficiently monitor and troubleshoot poor performance. To connect your metric, trace, and log data during configuration, you can [assign](https://docs.datadoghq.com/getting_started/tagging/assigning_tags) the relevant [reserved tags](https://docs.datadoghq.com/getting_started/tagging/#introduction) `host`, `device`, `source`, `service`, `env`, and `version` to the data. Reserved tags are specifically used in Datadog to correlate data types across the platform, and these tags should only be assigned for their defined purposes.

The reserved tags `service`, `env`, and `version` specifically allow for [unified service tagging](https://docs.datadoghq.com/getting_started/tagging/unified_service_tagging) so that you can navigate seamlessly across traces, metrics, and logs with consistent tags. To learn more about tagging, you can take the course [Tagging Best Practices](https://learn.datadoghq.com/courses/tagging-best-practices). In addition to assigning reserved tags, you can also [connect your logs to your traces](https://docs.datadoghq.com/tracing/connect_logs_and_traces/?tab=java) by injecting `traceid` and `spanid` tags from your trace data into your log data (as indicated in the image below of a log with correlated trace data). This will allow you to toggle between the related APM Trace Details and the Log Side Panel.

When you configure log collection, you can also try to configure out-of-the-box log processing.
