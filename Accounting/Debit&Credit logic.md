
| **English Term** | **Ukrainian Translation**           | **What It Represents**                                                                                                                                                                              | **How it Increases**         |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Assets**       | **Активи** _(Aktyvy)_               | **Things of value the company owns.** This includes physical cash, bank accounts (like `DCA`), and money other people owe you (Accounts Receivable, like the `ATM Open position`).                  | **Debit** (Positive number)  |
| **Liabilities**  | **Зобов'язання** _(Zobov'yazannya)_ | **Money the company owes to others.** This includes loans, pending payouts, and holding accounts where you collect money that belongs to a third party (like `ATM Forwarding` or `ATM Chargeback`). | **Credit** (Negative number) |
| **Equity**       | **Капітал** _(Kapital)_             | **The owner's net worth in the business.** It is whatever is left over if you subtract all your Liabilities from all your Assets.                                                                   | **Credit** (Negative number) |
| **Revenue**      | **Дохід** _(Dokhid)_                | **Money earned from doing business.** This is the income generated from operations, like the fees customers pay to use the ATM.                                                                     | **Credit** (Negative number) |
| **Expenses**     | **Витрати** _(Vytraty)_             | **The costs of running the business.** This includes rent, salaries, processing costs, and financial losses or write-offs (like the `ATM Delta` account when a chargeback is lost).                 | **Debit** (Positive number)  |

### When a Debit INCREASES the balance

For these two account types, a debit means you are adding more value (the number gets bigger):

- **Assets (Активи):** If you receive $100 cash, you **Debit** your cash account. The balance goes up from $0 to $100.
    
- **Expenses (Витрати):** If you pay $50 for internet, you **Debit** your internet expense account. Your total expenses for the month go up.
    

### When a Debit DECREASES the balance

For the other three account types, a debit means you are **reducing** the balance (the absolute number gets smaller):

- **Liabilities (Зобов'язання):** If you owe the bank $1,000 and you pay back $200, you **Debit** the loan account. Your debt decreases from $1,000 to $800.
    
- **Revenue (Дохід):** If you refund a customer $20, you **Debit** your revenue account. Your total earned revenue for the month goes down.
    
- **Equity (Капітал):** If the owner takes cash out of the business, you **Debit** equity, reducing the owner's stake.]
